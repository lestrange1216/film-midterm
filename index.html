📝 시험장 여백에 그릴 3열 필기 전략 (The 3-Column Note Template)

1st Screen: 개요 / 2nd Screen: 세부 디테일
📷 1. IMAGE (화면/미장센)
  • • Framing: CU, EWS, Medium Shot?
  • • Angle: Eye-level, Low, High, Canted?
  • • Movement: Handheld, Tracking, Pan, Tilt?
  • • Lighting: High-key, Low-key, Chiaroscuro?
  • • Color: Saturated, Desaturated, Palette?
  • • Mise-en-scène: Props, Costumes, Blocking?
✂️ 2. EDITING (편집)
  • • Cutting Rate: Fast cutting vs Long take?
  • • Pacing / ASL: Rapid panic vs Slow dread?
  • • Continuity: Eyeline match, Shot-reverse-shot?
  • • Disjunction: Jump cut, Elliptical jump?
  • • Rhythm: Cuts timed to physical/music beat?
  • • Transition: Cut, Dissolve, Fade, Graphic match?
🔊 3. SOUND (음향)
  • • Diegetic: Dialogue, screams, footsteps, Foley?
  • • Non-Diegetic: Orchestral score, jazz trumpet?
  • • Sound Bridge: Audio overlaps visual cut?
  • • Counterpoint: Ironic classical music over gore?
  • • Silence: Sudden acoustic drop, muting?
  • • Voiceover: Extradiegetic narrator, singing?

템플릿: Part II 고득점 답안 문장 빌더 (The Master Formula)

1. The Formal Claim (형식의 기능을 주장하는 첫 문장)

"In this sequence, [Director] utilizes [Formal Technique: e.g., low-key chiaroscuro lighting / an unbroken long take] to [Active Verb: e.g., externalize / destabilize / expose] [Narrative/Thematic Conflict: e.g., the protagonist's descent into moral ambiguity / systemic state negligence]."

2-4. Three Formal Elements Tied to Specific Moments (3가지 형식 요소 실증)

"First, regarding the visual image, the camera employs a [Specific Shot Scale & Angle: e.g., tight close-up at eye level] when [Specific Scene Moment], which [Formal Function: forces spectator identification with the character's unvarnished grief]. Second, the editing reinforces this psychological pressure through [Specific Editing Choice: e.g., a sudden acceleration in cutting rate / an elliptical jump cut], [Formal Function: rupturing spatial coherence to evoke kinetic panic]. Third, this formal orchestration is anchored acoustically by [Sound Element: e.g., an intrusive sound bridge / diegetic muffled breathing] that [Formal Function: bridges narrative spaces and heightens claustrophobia]."

5. Course Reading / Concept Integration (수업 논문 개념의 실질적 적용)

"This cinematic form directly enacts [Scholar]'s concept of [Theoretical Term: e.g., Christina Klein's 'deep crime' / Youngmin Choe's 'transient monument' / Giorgio Agamben's 'bare life'], demonstrating that the sequence's formal violence is not mere sensational spectacle, but a rigorous diagnosis of [Deeper Socio-Political Critique]."

6. Direct Answer to the Added Prompt Question (추가 질문에 대한 직접적 입장 표명)

"Therefore, in response to the question of whether [Prompt Question], the clip demonstrates that [Direct Stance: e.g., the film subverts rather than reproduces the convention], because the formal juxtaposition of [Formal Evidence A] against [Formal Evidence B] ultimately privileges [Core Takeaway]."
